# Rebirth content 0.1.35

The Reverie panel now uses the rebuilt Skills, Heritages, Rebirth and Glossary
layout, current icons and structured server progress. Human Bloodline progression
is independent of your Life Heritage choice. Paragon is retired; affected
characters retain their earned history and may choose a replacement Life Heritage
for free.

Bot Manager can invite your saved four companions together. **Bag upgrades** scans
their existing bags, and **Repair vendor** uses a nearby repair NPC and charges
money. Wardrobe and its menu button are removed.

Update the launcher to **2.3.1**, close WoW, then choose **Update**. Existing local
heirloom preparation is preserved. Skillful content is unchanged.
